What we keep.

No-prep veneers at KLOVE
  • Your enamel.Little to no reduction. Ultra-thin handmade porcelain bonds to the surface.
  • An easier visit.Usually no drilling, often no numbing.
  • Your smile in between.Usually no temporaries needed.
  • Your options later.Your natural tooth structure is largely preserved.
  • A natural look.Thin, translucent porcelain. Veneers that don't announce themselves.

What traditional prep removes.

Traditional veneers
  • A layer of enamel.Usually shaved down first.
  • Some comfort.Typically numbing and drilling.
  • Your smile in between.Temporaries are usually worn while the porcelain is made.
  • The way back.Generally irreversible.
  • Some subtlety.Can look bulkier if over-prepared.

No-prep is best for discoloration, chips, worn edges, small teeth and small gaps. Larger changes in shape or position may call for traditional veneers.

Not everyone is a candidate for no-prep veneers. Your consult and exam decide.

Questions people ask before they book.

What can no-prep veneers improve?

Discoloration that whitening cannot fix, chips, worn edges, uneven shapes, teeth that look small and some small gaps.

Will my veneers look natural?

Shade, shape and surface detail are planned around your face and the teeth next to them.

Do you have to shave my teeth?

Usually little to none with no-prep veneers. Some cases need minimal preparation. Dr. Adrianna reviews your options and alternatives before you decide.

How long do veneers last?

Many years, though not a lifetime. Your bite, hygiene, habits and regular care all matter, and repair or replacement may be needed eventually.

What does it cost?

The consult is $199, credited toward your smile design. Your written plan shows the full price before you decide, and right now veneers are $500 off each.

What happens at the first appointment?

Photographs, a scan, your smile designed on screen that day, and a written plan.

Am I a candidate?

An exam comes first. Tooth and gum health, your bite and your goals decide whether no-prep veneers, whitening, Invisalign or another approach fits best.
